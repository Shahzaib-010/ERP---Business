# Bike & Installment Business Management System

A requirements and implementation handoff for building a single-business
management application using the MERN stack.

## Documents

-   `01-product-requirements.md` --- confirmed scope, workflows, roles,
    rules, and open decisions.
-   `02-technical-architecture.md` --- recommended MERN architecture,
    security, storage, and domain model.
-   `03-data-model.md` --- proposed MongoDB collections and key
    relationships.
-   `04-implementation-roadmap.md` --- phased delivery plan and
    acceptance criteria.
-   `05-claude-code-instructions.md` --- project-level instructions for
    Claude Code.

## Important implementation rule

Treat requirements explicitly marked **Confirmed** as requirements.
Treat items marked **Recommended default** as proposed implementation
choices that can be adjusted. Treat **Open decision** items as
unresolved; do not silently invent business rules.

## MVP principle

Keep this focused on digitizing the current business records and daily
operations. Do not turn it into a full ERP, complex POS, delivery
platform, public marketplace, or multi-branch accounting suite.
